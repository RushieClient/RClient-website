RClient (Rushie Client) is a custom DDNet client based on Tater Client, with additional dummy tools, visual options and customizable controls.

  • Dummy position, speed and aim information.
  • Player hitboxes, weapon indicators and chat bubbles.
  • 45° aim, reduced sensitivity and spectator shortcuts.

Try RClient in your browser, or download it for Windows, Linux, macOS and Android. RClient and RClient Extra have separate releases and repositories.
